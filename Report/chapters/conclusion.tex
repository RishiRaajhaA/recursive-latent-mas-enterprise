\chapter{Conclusion and Scope for Further Research}
\fi

\label{chap.6}
In order to examine the computational effectiveness, latency, and dependability of autonomous language agents in stateful corporate workflows, this thesis presented and empirically assessed a Recursive Multi-Agent System that communicates through continuous latent representations. Latent ReAct increased Hard-tier goal accuracy to 50.8\% across 912 benchmark evaluations on the stateful $\tau^2$-Bench Retail domain, while Text ReAct's improvement was 45.9\%. Additionally, it produced 25.2\% less tokens, decreased mean latency by 26.1\%, increased clean terminal successes by 10\% (33 versus 30), and decreased policy violations from 13 to 10. Trajectory stabilization was empirically demonstrated by geometric analysis: Latent ReAct concentrated about 64\% of its total trajectory displacement in the initial recursive transition, with a statistically significant decrease in subsequent drift ($p = 1.9 \times 10^{-20}$). Additionally, the duplicate-filtered analysis revealed a contraction of almost 70\% ($p = 7.1 \times 10^{-15}$). Nevertheless, without suitable Lipschitz restrictions, the residual adapters do not mathematically provide fixed-point contractivity. At terminal depth, the latent representations maintained 15.2 effective dimensions, as opposed to 18.9 at initial depth. After multiple-hypothesis correction, statistical analysis revealed no significant correlation between the tested global geometric measures and task accomplishment, indicating that global, role-averaged representations by themselves are not sufficient to predict execution results. The evaluation encompassed 114 tasks in a single retail domain, and the mean goal-accuracy gain was small (+0.6 percentage points averaged across recursion levels and +2.0 percentage points at terminal depth). These results should be evaluated in the context of the study's constraints.and Latent ReAct's isolated tool-selection accuracy was worse than Text ReAct's (54.2\% versus 57.5\%; bootstrap 95\% CI for the difference: $[-5.1\%, -1.5\%]$). Future research should look into adaptive early-stopping techniques driven by representation drift, hybrid lexical-latent training, and evaluation across more enterprise areas.
